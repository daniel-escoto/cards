## Tunings applied

1. Soft multiway equity: `hu ** (1 + (n−1)·0.2)` instead of `hu ** n` (preflop, postflop range equity, all-in calls).
2. Premium short-circuit vs ≤2.5× opens: 88+, AK, AQ always continue and prefer a 3-bet before pot-odds × raiseGate.
3. Position + table size: widen BTN/CO, tighten UTG/early; full ring (7–9) tightens unopened ranges (facing-raise keeps only part of that tighten).
4. Tiny river bets: softer range discount so A-high does not overfold to ~1/10-pot.

Styles stay distinct: Grinder/Quick tight, Casual a sticky calling station, Pressure most aggressive. Free-fold remains 0%.
