## Code that inflates folds

1. **Multiway equity as `headsUpEquity ** opponentCount`** (`estimateComputerEquity`, and postflop `rangeEquity ** opponents`) — with 5 opponents, even strong hands look like trash. Explains JJ folding ~95% multiway to a min-raise and 50% of “strong” hands folding to ≤2.5x opens at 9-max.
2. **No position / no 9-max tighten** — `preflopTableWiden` only widens below 6 players; 9-max uses the same thresholds as 6-max (VPIP stays too high for full ring while still overfolding to raises).
3. **Facing-raise path stacks pot-odds × `preflopFacingRaiseGate`** — weak-to-medium hands overfold; premiums should short-circuit to 3-bet/call before that product.
4. **Postflop river `rangeDiscount`** (≈1.45 + betFrac×0.65 + 0.7) can crush mid-strength hands getting good prices (A-high still folds ~54% to a 1/10-pot bet).

Free checks never fold: both preflop and postflop `callAmount === 0` branches only check or bet.

## Verdict

**Too tight against aggression, too passive overall — not globally “nit.”**

VPIP sits at/above a solid regular; PFR and AF are far too low (limp/call station). Fold-to-open and especially folding strong hands to small opens multiway are the “bots fold too much” feel. Postflop they are slightly **sticky** to c-bets/turn bets (fold% below typical), not overfolders.

### Highest-impact tweaks

1. Soften multiway equity to something like `hu ** (1 + (n-1)*0.35)` (and the same for postflop `rangeEquity`) — expect fold-to-open down several points, strong-vs-minraise folds → near 0, more 3-bets → PFR up.
2. Premium short-circuit vs small opens (pairs 88+, AK/AQ always continue; prefer 3-bet) before pot-odds×gate — kills the JJ multiway fold bug.
3. Position + table-size thresholds (widen BTN/CO, tighten UTG and full 9-max by ~0.04–0.06 on the play threshold) — lowers 9-max VPIP toward 18%, raises late-position PFR.
